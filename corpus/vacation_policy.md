# Vacation Policy — Northbridge Analytics

**Version:** 3.2
**Last updated:** January 2026
**Responsible department:** Human Resources

## 1. Available days

All full-time employees accrue **15 business days of vacation per calendar year**, prorated during the first year based on the hire date.

## 2. Carryover

Unused days may be carried over from one year to the next up to a maximum of **5 days**. Any balance above that limit is forfeited on December 31 of each year, unless explicitly authorized by Human Resources.

## 3. Request and approval

Vacation requests must be submitted **at least 10 business days in advance** through the HR portal, and require approval from the direct manager before being considered confirmed.

## 4. Blackout period

No vacation requests will be approved between **December 15 and December 31**, except in exceptional cases approved by the Operations Director, due to the annual fiscal close.

## 5. Payout upon termination

Upon termination of employment, unused vacation days are paid out in cash together with the final paycheck, calculated based on the employee's current base salary.
