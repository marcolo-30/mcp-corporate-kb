# Frequently Asked Questions — Internal IT Support (Northbridge Analytics)

## How do I reset my corporate password?

Go to portal.northbridge.internal/reset and follow the verification flow using your corporate email. The reset link expires in **15 minutes**.

## How do I request a new or replacement device?

Open a ticket on the support portal under the "Hardware" category. Failure-related replacements are processed within **3 business days**; new equipment (for example, for a new hire) must be requested **at least 5 business days** before the start date.

## I'm having trouble connecting to the VPN, what should I do?

First check that your VPN client is updated to the latest version. If the problem persists, open a ticket under the "Connectivity" category with "High" priority.

## What are IT support's hours of operation?

Support hours are **Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.**, local time. Outside these hours, only incidents marked as "Critical" are handled through the on-call line.

## Can I install software that hasn't been approved by the company?

No. All software installations must be pre-approved by the Information Security team, in accordance with the Code of Conduct.
