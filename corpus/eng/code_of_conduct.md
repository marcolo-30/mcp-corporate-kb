# Code of Conduct — Northbridge Analytics

**Version:** 4.0
**Last updated:** January 2026

## 1. Non-discrimination

Northbridge Analytics prohibits any form of discrimination or harassment based on race, gender, sexual orientation, religion, disability, national origin, or any other characteristic protected by applicable law.

## 2. Whistleblower channel

The company maintains an **anonymous whistleblower channel** (ethics hotline), available 24/7, managed by an independent third party. All reports are investigated within a maximum of **20 business days** of receipt.

## 3. Conflicts of interest

Every employee must disclose in writing any actual or potential conflict of interest — including family relationships with vendors or competitors — within **10 business days** of the conflict arising.

## 4. Acceptable use of resources

The company's technology resources (email, laptop, software licenses) are intended primarily for work use. Reasonable and occasional personal use is permitted, as long as it does not interfere with job duties or violate any other company policy.

## 5. Consequences of non-compliance

Violations of this code may result in disciplinary measures ranging from a written warning to termination of employment, depending on the severity of the violation.
