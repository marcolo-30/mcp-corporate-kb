# Service Agreement — CloudTech Solutions Inc.

**Document type:** Cloud hosting services agreement
**Parties:** Northbridge Analytics ("the Client") and CloudTech Solutions Inc. ("the Provider")
**Signing date:** March 14, 2025

## Clause 3 — Term

This agreement has an initial term of **24 months** from the signing date, automatically renewable for successive 12-month periods unless either party provides notice otherwise.

## Clause 7 — Early termination

Either party may terminate this agreement early by providing **written notice at least 60 days in advance**. If the Client terminates early without justified cause, a penalty equal to **10% of the value of the remaining contract months** will apply.

## Clause 9 — Service level (SLA)

The Provider guarantees **99.9% monthly uptime**. For each percentage point below this threshold, a service credit equal to **5% of the value of the corresponding monthly invoice** will apply, cumulative up to a maximum of 25% of that invoice.

## Clause 12 — Confidentiality

Both parties agree to keep confidential the technical and commercial information shared during the term of this agreement, and for an additional period of **2 years** after its termination.
