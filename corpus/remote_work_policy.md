# Hybrid Work Policy — Northbridge Analytics

**Version:** 1.4
**Last updated:** March 2026
**Responsible department:** Human Resources

## 1. Work arrangement

Northbridge Analytics operates under a hybrid schedule: **3 days of in-office work and 2 days of remote work** per week, coordinated with the team's direct manager.

## 2. Core hours

Regardless of work arrangement, all employees must be available during **core hours, 10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m.**, in their local time zone, to ensure overlap between teams.

## 3. Home-office equipment stipend

The company provides a monthly stipend of **50 USD** (or local currency equivalent) to cover connectivity and ergonomics expenses for remote work days. This stipend is paid together with the monthly payroll and does not require submitting receipts.

## 4. Eligibility

This policy applies to all employees with at least **3 months of tenure**. Employees still in their probationary period must attend in person 5 days a week, unless an exception is approved by their manager.
