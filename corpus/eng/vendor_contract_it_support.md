# Service Agreement — HelpDesk Partners LLC

**Document type:** Level 2 IT help-desk support agreement
**Parties:** Northbridge Analytics ("the Client") and HelpDesk Partners LLC ("the Provider")
**Signing date:** September 2, 2025

## Clause 4 — Scope of service

The Provider will deliver **level 2** technical support for hardware, software, and connectivity incidents reported by the Client's employees, Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. (Client's local time).

## Clause 5 — Response times

Guaranteed response times by incident severity are:

- **Critical:** response within a maximum of **4 hours**
- **High:** response within a maximum of 8 hours
- **Medium:** response within a maximum of 24 hours
- **Low:** response within a maximum of 72 hours

## Clause 8 — Term and renewal

This agreement has a term of **12 months**, automatically renewable annually unless either party provides notice of non-renewal at least 45 days in advance.

## Clause 11 — Confidentiality

The confidentiality obligation over shared information remains in effect for **3 years** after termination of this agreement, regardless of the reason for termination.
