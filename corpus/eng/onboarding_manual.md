# Onboarding Manual — Northbridge Analytics

**Version:** 2.0
**Last updated:** February 2026

## 1. First day

Every new hire receives a preconfigured laptop and an assigned **"buddy"** — a peer from a different team who supports them informally during their first 4 weeks.

## 2. Probationary period

The probationary period lasts **90 calendar days** from the hire date. During this period, the direct manager must hold at least two formal performance check-ins: one at day 30 and another at day 75.

## 3. Benefits enrollment

New hires must complete benefits enrollment (health insurance, savings plan, life insurance) within the **first 30 days** of joining, through the HR portal. After that window, they must wait for the next open enrollment period, which occurs every July.

## 4. Mandatory training

Every new hire must complete the following mandatory training within their first two weeks:

- Information security and data handling
- Code of conduct and whistleblower channel
- Workplace health and safety
