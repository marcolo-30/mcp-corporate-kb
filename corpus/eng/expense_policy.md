# Expense Reimbursement Policy — Northbridge Analytics

**Version:** 2.1
**Last updated:** May 2026

## 1. Eligible expenses

Travel, meal, and transportation expenses incurred during approved work activities are eligible for reimbursement, provided the **original invoice or receipt** is submitted.

## 2. Daily limits

The meal reimbursement limit during business travel is **75 USD per day** (or local currency equivalent). Expenses exceeding this limit require additional justification and manager approval.

## 3. Pre-approval

Any individual expense over **500 USD** requires prior written approval from the direct manager before the expense is incurred, not after.

## 4. Reimbursement timeline

Once a complete expense report with supporting documentation is submitted, reimbursement is processed within a maximum of **15 business days**.

## 5. Non-reimbursable expenses

The following are not reimbursable: traffic fines, personal entertainment expenses, or alcohol purchases outside of formally approved corporate events.
